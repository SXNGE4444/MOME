# MOME brand and trademark guidelines

The source code in this repository is distributed under Apache License 2.0. That source-code license does not grant a license to use the MOME name, logo, project branding, or the identities of Last Dices or Blanco Studio as trademarks or as implied endorsements.

**Allowed without separate permission**
- Accurate attribution: "Built with MOME", "Forked from the MOME project" or equivalent factual statements.
- Links to the upstream project, license text and contributor documentation.
- Use of the words MOME in unambiguous editorial descriptions of the upstream software.

**Ask for permission first**
- Naming a modified commercial distribution "MOME" as if it were the official product.
- Reusing the official identity to market a third-party app, service or merchandise.
- Using Last Dices or Blanco Studio logos or marks to suggest endorsement.

Distributing a modified version is allowed under Apache-2.0; please give it a **distinct product name and visual identity** when needed to avoid confusion about origin. Preserve license and NOTICE obligations.

These guidelines do not assert that a trademark is formally registered; legal ownership and registration should be reviewed by the appropriate rights holders. These guidelines do not override rights granted by the Apache-2.0 license.
